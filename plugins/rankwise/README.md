# Rankwise

Rankwise measures how often AI search engines cite your brand. This plugin connects the Rankwise MCP server at `https://tryrankwise.com/api/mcp` and adds two skills.

- `audit-to-briefs` reads your scorecard, your citation gaps and your content opportunities. It drafts briefs for the biggest gaps. It asks before it approves a brief or spends credits.
- `read-share-of-voice` reads your AI share of voice, the competitor leaderboard, citations per engine, and the citations you won or lost.

You sign in to Rankwise the first time a tool runs. The plugin acts in one workspace and only sees data that workspace can see.
